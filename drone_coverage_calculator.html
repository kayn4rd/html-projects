Assumptions & limitations
  • Flat terrain, constant AGL, camera pointing straight down (nadir). Lens distortion, relief and gimbal tilt are ignored.
  • Footprints are rectangles aligned with the local flight heading: footprint = AGL × sensor dimension ÷ focal length.
  • Sensor height is derived from sensor width and the image aspect ratio (square pixels assumed).
  • Along-track overlap = (1 − photo spacing ÷ along-track footprint) × 100. Negative values mean gaps between photos.
  • "Sum of footprints" counts overlapped ground repeatedly; "unique coverage" is the geometric union of all footprints.
  • Coverage depth = number of footprints that contain a photo's centre point (including its own).
  • Flight time = path length ÷ speed. Take-off, acceleration, turns, wind and battery swaps are not modelled.
  • Footprint geometry says nothing about photogrammetric accuracy or 3D reconstruction quality; that also depends on texture, lighting, motion blur, camera calibration, control points and processing software.
